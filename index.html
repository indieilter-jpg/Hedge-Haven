Hedge & Haven

A cozy fence-building puzzle for iPhone: place a few garden fences, keep the night threats out and give your little friend the biggest safe garden you can.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to indieilter@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and the game version (Settings, About).

Your progress and purchases

Your progress is stored only on your iPhone; deleting the game deletes it unless it is restored from a device backup. If the game offers purchases, restore them in Settings, Gameplay, Restore purchases; refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
